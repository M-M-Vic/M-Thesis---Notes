\section{Results}\label{sec:results}

This section reports the numerical study underpinning the analysis. Every closed-form
expression derived above is first validated against an exact continuous-time Markov chain
(CTMC); the validated formulas are then used to compare the solved models and to quantify the
two mechanisms. To keep the focus on the principal findings, the exhaustive figure-by-figure
evidence --- the per-result validation maps, the simulation cross-checks, the parameter
sweeps, the class-asymmetry study, and the convergence diagnostics --- is collected in
Appendix~\ref{app:numerics}. The main text retains the validation verdict table, the
structural and numerical comparison tables, and the three plots that carry the central
conclusions.

\subsection{Validation against the CTMC}\label{subsec:validation}

Every closed-form expression in this thesis is validated numerically against a truncated CTMC
solver, which serves as the ground truth: it solves the linear system $\pi Q=0$ directly and
makes no approximation beyond the finite truncation at $N_{\max}=40$, whose effect is
controlled and small in the parameter regimes tested. Each theoretical result --- two lemmas,
three theorems, two corollaries, one approximation theorem, and two experiment-model theorems
--- is verified against the exact stationary distribution so obtained, at base parameters
$\lambda_1=0.3$, $\lambda_2=0.4$, $\mu=1.0$, with the mechanism parameter ($\gamma_1$ or
$\theta_1$) set to $0.5$ where applicable.

Table~\ref{tab:validation_summary} collects the verdict for every result. All mathematical
results are accepted to within CTMC truncation noise: the lemma residuals and the closed-form
PGF errors fall between $10^{-7}$ and $10^{-15}$ --- the gap reflecting numerical quadrature in
the integral-form models rather than formula error --- and the corollary scatter against the
CTMC lies on the diagonal to $10^{-4}$ across $60$ random configurations each. As a further,
assumption-free check, the CTMC ground truth is itself cross-validated against a discrete-event
simulation of the master chain, with agreement to the $O(1/\sqrt{n})$ sampling floor; this
closes the verification loop. The full visual evidence --- lemma-residual maps, $P(x,y)$ error
grids, boundary-function overlays, corollary scatter, and the simulation-versus-CTMC
comparisons --- is deferred to Appendix~\ref{app:numerics:validation}.

A single entry is only conditionally accepted. The partial-PGF recursion on the alternative
state space $\widetilde{S}$ (Approximation~\ref{apx:A:PPGF_Stilde}) is approximate by design:
it drops the boundary-diagonal forcing term, so it is exact at $y=1$ for all parameters but
degrades wherever that neglected forcing competes with the homogeneous solution. Numerically
it is reliable only in the priority-dominated, moderately loaded regime
($\rho_1/\rho\gtrsim0.6$, $\rho\lesssim0.6$) and inaccurate elsewhere; the validity map is
Figure~\ref{fig:val_ppgf} in Appendix~\ref{app:numerics:validation}. This is the one place
where the $\widetilde{S}$ coordinatisation fails to deliver an exact result, and it is recorded
as an approximation, not a theorem.

\subsection{Convergence to the baseline}\label{subsec:results:convergence}

Each specialised model must recover Model-$A$ as its distinguishing parameter vanishes; the
comparison above establishes this algebraically, and the CTMC pipeline confirms it numerically.
Measured in the $L_1$ distance $\lVert\pi-\pi_A\rVert_1$ between the full joint distributions,
all four specialised models --- $B_2$, $C_2$, and the head-of-line $\BH$, $\CH$ --- approach
the baseline at first order in their distinguishing parameter, with fitted log--log slopes of
$0.85$--$0.93$ (the slight shortfall below unity is a finite-truncation artefact of the fit,
not a genuine fractional rate). The rate is dictated by the fundamental equation: in each model
the mechanism term enters linearly in $\gamma_1$ or $\theta_1$, so the stationary distribution
is smooth at the origin and its first-order Taylor term governs the leading deviation. For the
conserving models $B_2$ and $\BH$ the empty-state descriptors $\pi_0$ and $\pi(0,0)$ equal
their Model-$A$ values \emph{identically} for every $\gamma_1>0$ (Corollaries~\ref{cor:B2:pi00},
\ref{cor:B21:pi0}), so the approach is carried entirely by the interior redistribution of mass
between the two coordinates. The full convergence diagnostics --- the per-descriptor rates, the
singular $\gamma_1\to0$ limit of $B_2$, the instant-jockeying limit $\gamma_1\to\infty$, the
fitted-rate table, and the synoptic dashboard --- are collected in
Appendix~\ref{app:numerics:convergence}.
